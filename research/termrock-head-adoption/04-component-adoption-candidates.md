## Dead ends and contradictions

- **`termrock::Theme` is deleted at HEAD** (guard test `lib.rs:123-124`: "Theme must be RolePalette (Break B)") while console `scroll_block.rs:36` and 351 further matching lines call `Theme::default()` (counted with `rg -n 'Theme::default' crates | wc -l`, 2026-08-19) — every pairing above sits behind the palette rename; chapter 03 owns that analysis.
- **Version numbering is internally inconsistent at HEAD**: workspace `Cargo.toml:13` still says `0.11.0`, git tags stop at `v0.9.0` (`git tag`), the pin describes as `v0.9.0-74-g5ff94ee1`, yet MIGRATING.md indexes entries through `v0.14.0`. "Adopt head" therefore means bumping the `rev` pin, not a released version; the `version = "=0.11.0"` field in jackin's `Cargo.toml:118` will be wrong-or-stale either way.
- **jackin-tui's charter vs upstream runtime**: `lib.rs:3-4` forbids the facade owning event loops, and `arch.rs:272-279` forbids `run.rs`/`terminal.rs`/`theme.rs` there — but jackin-tui `runtime.rs` already owns the `Component`/`View`/`Subscription` contracts that upstream `runtime`/`event_result` now cover. Both "keep product traits" and "adopt upstream contracts" satisfy the letter of the gate; they cannot both be the end state.
- **Patterns looked like components until read**: `patterns/*` headers self-describe as copy-adapt teaching recipes (`resource_browser.rs:17-18`). Any plan line reading "adopt patterns/settings_screen" is a category error; corrected throughout to "steal composition".
- `widgets/detail_table.rs` (pre-pin) overlaps ✚`key_value_table`/✚`data_table` at HEAD; which is upstream's own preferred survivor was not determinable from module headers alone — dropped rather than asserted.
